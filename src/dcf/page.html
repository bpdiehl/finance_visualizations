Operating forecast
From profit to cash flow
  1. Start with operating profit (EBIT). Leave out financing interest.
  2. Subtract tax on operating profit. This gives after-tax operating profit (NOPAT).
  3. Add back depreciation. It reduced accounting profit without using cash this year.
  4. Subtract capital spending and the increase in operating NWC. These investments use cash; a fall in NWC releases cash.

The remainder is operating free cash flow (FCF), available to both debt and equity providers.
