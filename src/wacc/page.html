Assumptions

Debt risk assumption

λ sets how strongly debt’s required return rises with debt share. λ = 0 holds debt return at rf; λ = 1 assigns more asset risk to debt. This is an illustrative schedule.

d = D/V and E/V = 1 − d are market-value weights; λ is dimensionless. All r values and WACC are annual required returns, not accounting ROA or promised payments. rf is the risk-free rate; T is the corporate tax rate.

Model scope and tax assumptions

The fixed pretax blend ra is an imposed teaching assumption. The debt schedule supplies the extra relationship needed to determine both investor returns. WACC falls here when d × rd increases and T > 0; with T = 0 it equals ra. This does not estimate a universal capital structure optimum or a leverage-invariant unlevered discount rate in a full corporate-tax valuation model. Distress costs, personal taxes, tax-loss limits and feedback from financing to market value are omitted. Debt return means expected required return, rather than a defaultable bond’s promised yield. At d = 0, rd is a hypothetical borrowing rate. The 90% debt limit preserves positive equity.
